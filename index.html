Main-drive construction management and troubleshooting on a deep power-tunnel TBM; technical review of a backfill-material change; productivity reporting with regular site feedback; TBM specification review, launching and initial-drive support; stability review for a shaft back-tunnel extension; productivity and risk review for an overseas deep-rail TBM; a two-week site OJT. Research

Selected research

01

Two-component backfill grout for shield TBM tail void

Master's thesis. Characterized grout properties — flow, gel time, plasticity, compressive strength, bleeding, viscosity — and evaluated injection performance on a 1:8 scaled tunnel model. Hardener (Component B) dosage and water-to-cement ratio produced consistent property trends, and four proposed metrics — watertightness, fillability, homogeneity, soundness — give quantitative criteria for selecting a shield-TBM backfill grout to suit field conditions.
